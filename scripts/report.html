1. Executive Architectural Summary & Core Tenets

The POS Retail SaaS Backend has been completely implemented as a robust, secure, and performant Modular Monolith designed specifically for the Pakistani retail, apparel boutique, and fabric market. The system strictly fulfills all enterprise requirements:

1.1 Zero-Floating-Point Financial Math (BigInt Minor Units)

In accordance with strict accounting and Pakistani fiscal standards, all monetary values across the entire database, services, and transactions are stored as 64-bit integer BigInt paisas (1 PKR = 100 paisas). This completely eliminates IEEE-754 binary floating-point roundoff errors during subtotal aggregation, discount splits, GST calculations, and ledger balance reconciliation. Standardized utilities paisaToRupees(bigint): number and rupeesToPaisa(number): bigint bridge database minor units with presentation layer JSON numbers.

1.2 Single-Tier Direct Store Tenancy

Following the comprehensive architectural refactoring, redundant branch hierarchies were eliminated. All entities—including POS counters, catalog products, inventories, cashier shifts, orders, expenses, and campaigns—link directly to the Store (storeId). Every database query and write operation strictly enforces compound scoping:

// Dual-scoping pattern enforced across all 16 modules to eliminate IDOR: await this.prisma.productVariant.findFirst({ where: { id: variantId, storeId: currentTenantStoreId } });

1.3 Timezone & Fiscal Normalization (Asia/Karachi)

All date math, calendar day boundaries (00:00:00 to 23:59:59), month ranges, and aging calculations are centralized through @core/utils/date-time.util.ts. Operating in Pakistan Standard Time (Asia/Karachi, UTC+5) ensures that daily sales totals, end-of-day register closing balances, and month-to-date reports remain identical whether viewed by the cashier in Lahore or the store owner remotely.

1.4 Defense-in-Depth Security & IDOR Elimination

Every non-public controller is safeguarded with @UseGuards(JwtAuthGuard, RolesGuard) and role-based permissions (Role.OWNER, Role.MANAGER, Role.CASHIER). Cashier PINs are securely hashed via bcrypt (10 salt rounds), stored in pinHash, and verified in constant time.

3. In-Depth Analysis: Newly Completed Modules & POS Parity

3.1 Khata & Receivables (/api/v1/khata)

The Khata module provides complete credit management tailored for Pakistani apparel retail credit customers ("Udhaar"):

  • Dynamic Aging Engine: Calculates receivables across 4 aging buckets: Current (Within Terms), Overdue 1-30 Days, Overdue 31-60 Days, and Overdue 61+ Days based on the oldest unsettled debit purchase ledger entry.
  • Slide-Over Account Drawer: Fetches complete chronological ledger entries showing both credit purchases (debits) and payment receipts (credits) with running balance in BigInt paisas.
  • Payment Collection: Supports partial and full repayments with automatic ledger balancing and payment receipts.
  • WhatsApp Payment Reminders: Formats polite Urdu/English reminder messages with exact overdue amounts and bank details, persisting message logs into WhatsAppMessageLog.
  • CSV Statement Export: Streams full aging accounts into RFC-4180 compliant CSV format.

3.2 WhatsApp Marketing & Campaigns (/api/v1/whatsapp)

Designed to leverage WhatsApp as the primary communication and marketing channel for boutiques in Pakistan:

  • Broadcast Campaigns: Allows store owners to launch and schedule broadcast campaigns targeted by customer segment (Gold Tier, Silver Tier, Inactive > 60 days, or All Customers).
  • Credit Balance Tracking: Automatically checks store message credit balances and deducts credits per sent broadcast.
  • Template Management: Pre-loads approved Meta message templates (e.g., Seasonal Eid Collection, VIP Loyalty Offer, Payment Reminder).
  • Automated Trigger Rules: Configurable toggles for automated digital receipts, post-purchase review requests, and back-in-stock notifications.
  • Delivery Logs & Instant Retry: Real-time message logs with status tags (DELIVERED, FAILED, READ) and 1-click retry.

3.3 Reports & Business Analytics (/api/v1/reports)

Provides 4 comprehensive reporting tabs aggregating real-time database figures:

  • Sales Analytics Tab: Net revenue, COGS, gross profit margins, average order value, payment method breakdown (Cash vs Card vs Bank vs Khata), and cashier sales performance rankings.
  • Inventory Valuation & Dead-Stock Tab: Total inventory valuation at cost vs retail price, potential margin, turnover velocity, and automated detection of dead-stock variants (zero sales in 90+ days) with tied-up capital calculations.
  • Finance & P&L Tab: Net profit/loss, operating expenses categorized by vendor and utility, cash drawer in/out movements, and receivables/payables position.
  • Customers RFM Tab: Recency, frequency, and monetary analysis of top VIP spenders and loyalty redemption rates.
  • CSV Export Streaming: High-performance CSV export across all 4 reporting domains.

3.4 Operational Settings (/api/v1/settings)

Unified 5-tab settings engine matching 100% of the frontend settings design system:

  • Store Profile: Legal business name, tagline, category, primary phone, WhatsApp contact, street address, operating hours, NTN, STRN, and FBR POS integration toggles.
  • Staff & Permissions: Team directory with role assignment (OWNER, MANAGER, CASHIER), terminal binding, max discount thresholds, and bcrypt-hashed 4-digit POS PINs.
  • Billing & Subscription: Plan tier (Starter, Professional, Enterprise), counter usage metrics, saved payment methods, and historical invoices.
  • Receipt Printer Customization: Paper size (80mm thermal vs 58mm vs A4), auto-print toggles, cash drawer kick on cash checkout, barcode printing, header/branch info, and return policy footer.
  • Sales, Stock & Credit Rules: Negative inventory prevention, required phone toggle, auto-rounding to nearest Rupee, return window (e.g. 14 days), low stock alert thresholds, default Khata credit limits, and overdue credit blocking.

3.5 Cashier Shift Drawer Parity (POST /api/v1/shifts/cash-movement)

Allows cashiers and managers to record drawer cash injections and removals (CASH_IN for additional morning change, CASH_OUT for vendor cash payouts or bank deposits) during an open shift. Expected drawer cash updates atomically to ensure flawless end-of-day register reconciliation.
